# Lens: research and product direction

## Decisions that hide here
- The actual question: what would change a decision if answered.
- Method: computational screening, physical test, literature review, or a combination, and in what order.
- Test standards that apply and where deviating from them is acceptable.
- Sample size and what result would be convincing to the intended reader (a reviewer, a regulator, a customer, an internal team).
- What would change your mind: the result that would kill the idea.
- Who the work is for, and the smallest version that is useful to them.
- What to build next versus defer, and what is blocked on what.
- Ownership and disclosure: what is shared publicly, what stays internal, what needs protecting before it is shown.

## Red flags to raise even if not asked
- A plan with no result that would count as failure.
- Work that depends on data or access not yet secured.
- Publishing or showing something before protection decisions are made.

## Sketch style
Simple flow or timeline: the steps, the decision points, and where results feed back. Or a two-axis map (for example effort against value) placing the options. Keep to five or fewer labels.

## Default criteria
Value of the answer, time to first result, cost, credibility to the intended reader, risk.
